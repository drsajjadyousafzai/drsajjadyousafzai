insideBody: "The veins in your legs carry blood back up to your heart. Small valves inside them stop the blood falling back down. When these valves stop working, blood pools in the lower leg. Over time this shows as swelling, aching and the twisted veins you can see under the skin."
signs:
  - "Legs that feel heavy and ache by the end of the day"
  - "Swelling around the ankle that is worse in hot weather or after standing"
  - "Twisted, rope-like blue or green veins you can see or feel"
  - "Itching, or a brown or hard patch of skin near the ankle"
  - "In later stages, a wound near the ankle that is slow to heal"
treatment: "For many people the vein is closed from the inside using laser (a thin fibre that seals the faulty vein so blood flows through healthy veins instead). This is usually done while you are awake, with the leg numbed, and you go home the same day. Larger or more advanced veins may need a small operation. Dr. Sajjad will examine your legs, often with an ultrasound scan, and tell you which option fits you."
