insideBody: "The veins in your legs carry blood back up to your heart. Small valves inside them stop the blood falling back down. When these valves stop working, blood pools in the lower leg. Over time this shows as swelling, aching and the twisted veins you can see under the skin."
signs:
  - "Legs that feel heavy and ache by the end of the day"
  - "Swelling around the ankle that is worse in hot weather or after standing"
  - "Twisted, rope-like blue or green veins you can see or feel"
  - "Itching, or a brown or hard patch of skin near the ankle"
  - "In later stages, a wound near the ankle that is slow to heal"
treatment: "There are three main ways a faulty vein can be treated, and Dr. Sajjad will examine your legs, usually with an ultrasound scan, to tell you which one fits your case. EVLA (endovenous laser treatment) closes the vein from the inside using a thin laser fibre, done with the leg numbed while you stay awake, and you go home the same day. Ultrasound-guided foam sclerotherapy uses an ultrasound scan to guide an injection of medical foam into the faulty vein, closing it without any cut, and suits smaller or more winding veins. Surgery to remove the vein is used for larger or more advanced veins, or when the vein is not suited to laser or foam."
recovery:
  - "Day of treatment: you can walk straight away, and walking is encouraged."

  - "Heavy physical work may need a little longer. You will be given a clear plan."
