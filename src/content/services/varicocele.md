insideBody: "A varicocele is a group of swollen veins in the scrotum, a little like varicose veins in the leg. The blood does not drain away properly and pools there. This can raise the temperature around the testicle, which is one reason it can affect the making of healthy sperm and cause fertility problems."
signs:
  - "A dull ache or dragging feeling in the scrotum, worse after standing all day"
  - "A soft lump that feels like a bag of worms, often on the left side"
  - "One side of the scrotum looking or feeling larger"
  - "Difficulty with fertility, found during tests for a couple trying for a baby"
treatment: "The swollen veins are tied off so blood drains the correct way. Dr. Sajjad does this under an operating microscope (a special magnifying microscope used during surgery). The magnification lets him find and protect the tiny artery and lymph vessels, which lowers the chance of problems and gives a cleaner result. It is a small operation and most men go home the same day."
recovery:
  - "First few days: rest, with mild soreness managed by simple pain medicine. Supportive underwear helps."
  - "One week: most men return to office work and light activity."
  - "Two to four weeks: avoid heavy lifting and hard exercise until cleared."
  - "If the operation was for fertility, a sperm test is usually repeated after a few months to check improvement."
