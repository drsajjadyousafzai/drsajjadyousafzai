insideBody: "Arteries carry fresh blood down to your legs. When they become narrowed or blocked, usually by a slow build-up over years, the muscles cannot get enough blood while you walk. The muscle cramps and forces you to stop. After you rest, the pain eases, then returns when you walk again."
signs:
  - "Cramp or ache in the calf or thigh after walking a set distance"
  - "The pain easing within a few minutes of stopping"
  - "Cold feet, or one foot colder than the other"
  - "Slow-healing sores on the foot or toes"
  - "In advanced cases, pain in the foot even at rest, especially at night"
treatment: "Treatment starts with medicine, stopping smoking, and a walking programme, which together can improve things a great deal. When the narrowing is severe, the artery can be widened from the inside using a balloon, and a small metal tube called a stent can be placed to hold it open (this is called angioplasty and stenting). Dr. Sajjad will use scans to decide what is right for you."
recovery:
  - "After a balloon or stent: this is often a day case or a short stay, with a small puncture, not a large cut."
  - "First week: light walking as advised, avoiding heavy strain."
  - "Ongoing: medicine and a walking routine protect the result. Managing diabetes, blood pressure and stopping smoking all matter."
faqs:
  - question: "Is angioplasty a big operation?"
    answer: "No. Angioplasty is usually done through a small puncture in the skin, not a large cut. Many patients go home the same day or after a short stay."
  - question: "Can leg pain when walking be treated without surgery?"
    answer: "Often yes. Medicine, stopping smoking and a steady walking programme improve many patients. Surgery or a balloon is used when the narrowing is severe or the simpler steps are not enough."
  - question: "What happens if I ignore it?"
    answer: "Untreated, the blood supply can worsen, leading to rest pain, wounds that will not heal, and in the worst cases the risk of losing part of the limb. Seen early, it is much easier to manage."

Cramping leg pain when walking is often brushed off as old age. It is frequently a treatable artery problem, and treating it early protects the limb.
